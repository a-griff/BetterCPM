BETTERCP/M NOAH ARCHIVE UTILITY

Keep NOAH.COM and its matching NOAH.OVL together. Change to the Utilities
Disk before running NOAH (e.g. B:), then qualify archive/source operands
with the desired writable disk and user. Overlay lookup uses the current
invocation DU, then A0:; it does not search arbitrary disks or users.

NOAH /HELP
NOAH /LIST C:FILES.ARK
NOAH /TEST C:FILES.ARK
NOAH /EXTRACT C:FILES.ARK D:
NOAH /ADD C:FILES.ARK D:*.TXT
NOAH /ADD /SQ C:TEXTS.ARK D:*.TXT
NOAH /ADD /CR C:BINARY.ARK D:*.BIN
NOAH /REMOVE C:FILES.ARK *.BAK
NOAH /COMPACT C:FILES.ARK

LBR supports the same operations. /CAPACITY=n controls LBR directory
capacity on ADD/COMPACT. Unknown new extensions need /FORMAT=LBR or
/FORMAT=ARK. Existing files are recognized by content, never converted.
Native ARC output is stored method 2, /SQ method 4 or /CR method 8, with
the original member name. ARC reading supports historical methods 1-9.
LBR compressed members use standalone SQ/CR streams and marked names.
Standalone /SQ, /CR and /EXPAND remain available; /LZ is reserved.

/Y approves ordinary replacements/removals and explicit COMPACT. It does
not override read-only/system protection. /R and /SYS enable separate
protection questions. /Q suppresses routine progress, preserving warnings,
failures, choices and the final outcome. ESC/Ctrl-C cancels at safe bounds.

All output is staged, closed, reopened and verified before publication.
Originals are rechecked; replacement uses private TMP/BAK names. Keep any
reported recovery files until their contents and the final path are known.
If rollback is uncertain, the batch stops and reports the actual paths.
This is not power-loss-atomic filesystem recovery; no automatic resume is
provided. Sources and CP/M record bytes (including 00h/1Ah) are preserved.

Ordinary ARC updates preserve retained headers/payloads and the entire
suffix after the end marker. ARC COMPACT trims excess 00h/1Ah padding;
it refuses opaque suffix bytes rather than deleting possibly useful data.
COMPACT never recompresses retained members. No-match REMOVE is unchanged.

Selection is frozen, up to 512 candidates; actual transaction capacity
also depends on active TPA, source plans and codec workspaces. Low-memory
requests fail before the archive is changed. ZIP, LZH/CrLZH and the
interactive archive browser remain future work.

Inline help: ordinary replacement offers Y/N/A/C/? (default No).
? explains: Y Yes, N No, A All, C Cancel, ? Help.
Safe recovery offers R/S/A/? (default Skip).
? explains: R Retry, S Skip, A Abort all, ? Help.
Help repeats the same item without changing policy or files. /Y never waits;
/Q retains required prompts/help. Simple Y/N and paging are unchanged.
